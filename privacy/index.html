Privacy

This page describes how the Anatoly Mazo website treats information. It is intentionally short because the site does not run a hosted form backend or sell data.

Who operates this site

This website is operated by Anatoly Mazo, an independent compliance consultant. It is not a law firm, not a registered investment adviser, and not affiliated with or endorsed by Wells Fargo.

Contact

Consultation requests go by email to anatolymazo@gmail.com. The on-site form does not transmit your details to a third-party form processor; it opens your own email application with a pre-filled message. If you send a message, the contents of that email are used only to respond to your inquiry.

What this site does not collect

This site does not use a newsletter signup, does not require an account, and does not ask for payment information. No office address or telephone number is published because none is offered as a public contact channel.

Hosting and logs

The host that serves this site may keep ordinary technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability. Those logs are controlled by the host, not used here for advertising.

Questions

Email anatolymazo@gmail.com if you want a message removed from the inbox or have a privacy question.
